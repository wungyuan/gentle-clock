Gentle Clock Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

In short: Gentle Clock does not collect any data. We have no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs. Everything you create stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

  • Alarms, timers, sequences and settings.
  • Sounds you record or import (for example a family member's voice or a song clip).
  • Your approximate location or chosen city, used only to calculate sunrise and sunset times.

This data is never sent to us or anyone else. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone, which is handled by Apple under your settings.

Permissions

  • Alarms: so your alarms can ring, even in Silent mode or a Focus.
  • Notifications: for timer and trial-ending reminders.
  • Microphone: only while you record a sound yourself.
  • Location (optional, while using the app): to calculate sunrise and sunset for your area. You can choose a city manually instead. The built-in city list works offline.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. The app only checks on your device whether you own Pro; we never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
